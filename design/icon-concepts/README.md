# Propositions d’icône projo

Quatre pistes exploratoires générées avec l’outil intégré image_gen, à partir de design/README.md, des artboards et des styles de l’application. Elles ne sont pas intégrées à l’application.

## Monogramme p.

Fichier : 01-monogramme.png

Prompt :

Use case: logo-brand. Create ONE polished square app icon concept for projo, a personal movies and TV media app. Visual basis: existing projo UI uses near-black #0A0A0B, off-white #F4F4F5, Geist lowercase bold typography, generous rounded corners, restrained smoked-glass surfaces and subtle inset rim light. Direction 1: a bold custom lowercase p with a separate small round full stop at its lower right, echoing the wordmark projo. Make the p a beautifully proportioned simple geometric typographic silhouette, large open round counter, short descender, soft but precise corners. Warm white solid mark on charcoal-black rounded-square tile, very restrained satin depth in tile only. Quiet, iconic, memorable at 24 pixels. Front-facing orthographic 2D graphic, centered, mark occupying 58 percent of tile width, generous padding. Tile nearly fills square canvas with tiny neutral dark margin. No full brand word, no label, no additional lettering, no device mockup, no decoration, no neon or vivid colors, no watermark. Exactly one icon.

## Projection

Fichier : 02-projection.png

Prompt :

Use case: logo-brand. Create ONE polished square app icon concept for projo, a personal cinema and TV app. Match this existing UI design language: near-black #0A0A0B background, off-white #F4F4F5 accents, smoked glass, restrained luminous edges, soft pill geometry, minimal and cinematic. Direction 2: an abstract projector beam, a small white luminous circular source at left emitting a broad short rounded triangular cone toward the right. Distill projection into two bold geometric elements; the beam should be a strong elegant wedge with softly rounded corners, an off-white frosted-glass material and subtle fading light inside its shape. It must have a crisp readable silhouette at small scale. No physical projector body, no film reel. Place the symbol centered on a deep graphite rounded-square app tile, front-facing, generous padding, beam fills about 60 percent of tile width. Predominantly monochrome, no colored gradients, no rainbow, no overly glossy 3D, no stars, no letters, no labels, no wordmark, no device mockup, no watermark. Exactly one icon, square composition.

## Portail

Fichier : 03-portail.png

Prompt :

Use case: logo-brand. Create ONE polished square app icon concept for projo, a personal media app for movies and TV. Its existing UI is dark, monochrome, Geist typography, #0A0A0B backgrounds, #F4F4F5 accents, rounded panels and subtle smoked glass. Direction 3: a distinctive rounded cinema portal. A thick off-white rounded rectangular doorway or screen frame, nearly square but slightly taller, with a bold right-pointing rounded play triangle cut out of its dark inner area; use a small intentional interruption in the right frame edge aligned with the triangle point to suggest entering cinema. Strong balanced graphic silhouette, one coherent logo, enough thickness to work at 24 pixels, substantial negative space, no hairlines. Off-white mark, charcoal app tile, subtle satin rim on tile, completely front facing, minimal vector-like precision. Icon centered occupying 60 percent of tile width, ample clear space. No lettering, no title, no full brand name, no multiple concepts, no device mockup, no neon, no colored accent, no ornamental glow, no watermark. Exactly one icon.

## Cadres

Fichier : 04-cadres.png

Prompt :

Use case: logo-brand. Create ONE polished square app icon concept for projo, a personal cinema library app. Base visual language on existing app UI: near-black #0A0A0B, graphite #141416, off-white #F4F4F5, softly rounded corners, smoked-glass panels and a clean editorial feel. Direction 4: an abstract cinematic library made of exactly three short broad rounded widescreen frames, offset diagonally from back upper-left to front lower-right, compact balanced silhouette. Back two frames are filled graphite and medium silver, front frame is bright off-white with a small clear dark right-pointing play triangle cut out. Only three layers, large simple forms and clean separation to remain legible at small sizes. Flat front-facing geometry with very restrained frosted-glass edge lighting, no tilted perspective. Centered symbol on charcoal rounded-square tile, occupies 60 percent of tile width with generous padding. Strong monochrome identity, premium but quiet. No text, no lettering, no wordmark, no extra symbols, no film perforations, no vibrant colors, no neon, no device mockup, no watermark. Exactly one icon, square canvas.
