## Art direction and export

Photographic cinematic scenes with characters and implied stories, matching Projo's restrained dark UI: natural textures, deep layered shadows, amber practical light against cooler ambient tones, subtle grain and soft halation. Genre variety includes a lighter summer family scene. Compositions leave space for home-screen text while keeping readable silhouettes in thumbnails. No lettering, logos or player controls are baked into the images. No zoom animation in the preview.

All assets are opaque WebP. Full images use quality 86, thumbnails 82, timeline thumbnails 80 (libwebp, compression level 6). Native ImageGen output is 1672 × 941; exports are resized to 16:9 without creative retouching. Nord's additional shots used its original frame as a continuity reference.

These assets live separately from login/onboarding backgrounds. The app's existing catalog uses server-provided artwork; this pack and preview do not alter real media metadata or introduce fictional titles into a real library.
